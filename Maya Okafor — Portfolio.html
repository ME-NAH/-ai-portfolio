I started in print, moved into product design, and now spend most days in code. Working across all three means fewer handoffs and fewer surprises.

I like projects where the problem is a little messy: a dashboard nobody trusts, a brand that outgrew its logo, a form that loses people halfway. I work closely with small teams, write down what we decide, and prefer a working prototype to a long deck.

Away from the screen I bake bread, cycle along the Tagus, and collect old timetables.
